Place your NASDAQ ITCH 5.0 binary file in this folder.

Example filename: 01302020.NASDAQ_ITCH50

Then open main.go and update this line at the top:

    itchFilePath = `data\01302020.NASDAQ_ITCH50`

Change the filename to match whatever file you placed here.

Download ITCH files free from:
https://emi.nasdaq.com/ITCH/Nasdaq%20ITCH/
